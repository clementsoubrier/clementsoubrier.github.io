Research

My research focusses on mathematical biology and optimal transport.

  • I develop and analyse mathematical model to study biological phenomena, using dynamical systems, optimal transport and stochastic processes.
  • I also study optimal transport based metrics invariant under specific symmetries and develop algorithm to compute them.
